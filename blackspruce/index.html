A NIGHT IN THE WET WOODS

Your car left the road somewhere in the spruce. The phone is dead. The rain hasn't stopped for hours.

All you have is a torch with a tired battery. Somewhere out there is a road, and a town.

Something else is out there too. It only moves when you aren't looking at it.

Headphones recommended.
