Vanta Drift

SECTOR RUN · HORIZONTAL SHOOTER
SLIDE HERE OR ON THE SCREEN TO FLY

Slide a finger anywhere on the screen, or on the pad below it, and the ship follows your finger exactly. It fires automatically while you touch. Turn your phone sideways for full screen. Hold CHARGE and let go for a piercing beam. POD launches or recalls your drone.

Controller (PS5, Xbox and others): left stick or d-pad to fly, X or R2 to fire, hold Square or L2 to charge, Circle or L1 for the pod, Options to pause.

Move ←↑↓→/WASD · Fire Z/Space · Hold and release X to charge a beam · C launches or recalls the pod · P pause · M music · F full screen · G switch 3D / classic 2D graphics · V switch side view / cockpit view · H scoreboard. Orange carriers drop capsules: L laser, P plasma, M magma, S shield, D drone, B speed.
