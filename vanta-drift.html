Put your thumb on the joystick pad to fly. Your ship fires automatically while you steer. Turn your phone sideways for full screen. Hold CHARGE and let go for a piercing beam. POD launches or recalls your drone.

Controller (PS5, Xbox and others): left stick or d-pad to fly, X or R2 to fire, hold Square or L2 to charge, Circle or L1 for the pod, Options to pause.
