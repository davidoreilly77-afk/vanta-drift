The route

Four sectors. Four bosses.

Each sector has its own skies, its own enemies and its own acid house soundtrack. Clear the boss, spend your coins at the alien shop, and fly on to something nastier.

SECTOR 01

Violet Reach

A purple nebula over sleeping alien cities. Fighters, gun turrets and the first gunship.

DARK ACID · 128 BPM

Two ways to fly

Side-on or straight from the cockpit.

Play the classic way, threading a cave with enemies streaming in from the right. Or switch to the cockpit and fly down the canyon as they come straight at you.

  • Real 3D ships with live reflections, bloom and lighting from every shot and explosion
  • A classic 2D mode for older devices, one tap away
  • Every weapon and power-up works in both views
Break the core

Earn the fireworks.

Every sector ends with a giant biomechanical boss. It tracks you with its eyes, opens its jaws to fire and calls in fighters. Only the glowing core takes damage.

  • Well done with fireworks and a sector bonus when you win
  • Glip's Bazaar where the coins you collect buy ships, shields and upgrades
  • A scoreboard for your best runs, with three-letter pilot tags
  • Five lives' worth of chances to get one more sector than last time
How to play

Pick it up in seconds.

Your ship fires on its own while you steer. Hold charge for a bigger shot, and let the power-ups do the rest.

Touch

FLY
Slide a finger anywhere. The ship follows it exactly and fires as you steer.
CHARGE
Hold the big button, then let go.
POD
Launch or recall your drone.
FULL SCREEN
Turn your phone sideways.

Play it like an app

Full screen, no address bar.

Add Vanta Drift to your home screen and it opens full screen with its own icon. No download, no app store.

iPhone and iPad (Safari)

  1. Tap PLAY NOW to open the game.
  2. Tap the ≡ or Share button by the address bar.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, with Open as Web App on.
  4. Launch it from the new icon and turn sideways.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap PLAY NOW to open the game.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu.
  3. Choose Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Or just tap FULL in the game.
